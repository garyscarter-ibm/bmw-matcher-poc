# Vauxhall — phrase bank

Verbatim lines only, grouped. Punctuation, casing, dash type and unit spacing are reproduced as
found, because they are the evidence. Every entry carries its source and date.

**Fidelity rule for this file:** an unmarked line was returned as a complete line by the
fetcher and is exact. `[TRUNCATED]` means exact as far as it goes, then cut by the fetcher's
125-character quote cap; the remainder is unrecovered. `[PARAPHRASE]` means the words are the
fetcher's, not the brand's, and the line is included only for structure. See
`pages/00-READ-THIS-FIRST-fidelity-caveat.md`.

Source keys: `PR-date` = press release at `media.stellantis.com/uk-en/vauxhall/press/...`;
`STLA` = `stellantis.com/en/brands/vauxhall`; `APP` = App Store listing; `ASA` = asa.org.uk.

### 2a. Boilerplate variant, PR-2026-07-13 (Mokka GSE press information)

Fetched 2026-08-14 on a pointer from the visual agent. Heading is identical: `ABOUT VAUXHALL
MOTORS`. Two findings.

**First, more of sentence two recovered than any earlier pass returned:**

> Today, as the oldest and most familiar of Britain's automotive manufacturers, its philosophy is to build vehicles that `[TRUNCATED]`

So the sentence's subject is `its philosophy`, i.e. the brand refers to itself in the third
person and possessive even in its own boilerplate. Sentence (b) of the block is a
**philosophy statement**, not a claim.

**Second, the Team GB sentence is ABSENT from this variant.** Sentences (a), (b), (c), (d), (f)
and (g) are all present and identical in wording to the PR-2026-05-14 version, but the fetcher
confirmed the page `contains no sentence about Team GB or Los Angeles 2028`. So the boilerplate
is **modular, not fixed**: the Team GB and Los Angeles 2028 beat is included on some releases
(2026-05-14, 2026-08-05, 2026-05-06, 2026-07-16) and dropped on others (2026-07-13). Do not
describe the block as a single invariant paragraph.

`Bold & Pure` is the design-language name, written with an ampersand and initial caps on both
words, and it appears as both `design language` and `design philosophy` in one document. The

### 2c. Embargo line format, PR-2026-07-13

> EMBARGO: Driving Impressions – Wednesday, 22nd July @ 00:01 (UK)

A fifth date format: day name, comma, ordinal numeral, month, `@`, 24-hour time, country in
brackets. Press-trade furniture rather than brand copy, but it is first-party and it evidences
the ALL-CAPS-label-plus-colon habit.
