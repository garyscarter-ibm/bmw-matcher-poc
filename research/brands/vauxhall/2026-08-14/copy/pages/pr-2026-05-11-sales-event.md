Conventions extracted:
- **Finance tables carry two decimal places always** (`£27,045.00`, `£130.93`), while body and
  bullet prices are round and unpenced (`£1,000`, `£32,995`, `£27,495`).
- `On The Road Price` is Title Cased in full in the table, abbreviated `OTR` elsewhere.
- The term is expressed twice over: `47 Monthly Payments` plus

- Ampersand inside table labels (`inc. ECG & Sales Event offer`), `inc.` and `excl.`
  abbreviated with full stops.

Conventions extracted:
- `T&Cs apply.` is the abbreviated form, ampersand, no spaces, capital T and C.
- **Ordinal long-form dates in prose and small print** (`8th to 31st May`,
  `30th June 2026`, `1st July 2025 to 30th June 2026`). The vans release of 2026-05-14 uses
  **slashed numeric dates** (`08/05/2026 and 31/05/2026`) in its terms, so both formats are in
  use inside the legal register.
- VAT status always stated in brackets, and both spellings appear:
  `(incl. VAT)` and `(excl. VAT)`.
- `Retailers`, not "dealers". `Retailer` is the house word.
- Channel exclusions are named as `Motability`, `Fleet`, `Light Commercial Vehicle`,
  `Private Retail channel`.
- Geography excluded by abbreviation: `GB Mainland only`, `excludes NI, IoM and Channel
  Islands`. No Oxford comma in that list.
- Sub-unit pricing as `7p per kWh`; energy as `2,500 kWhs` with a plural s on the unit.
- A named competitor retailer is called out by name as non-participating
  (`Arnold Clark sites are not participating retailers.`), which is unusually specific for
  brand small print and is direct evidence of how granular the caveats get.
