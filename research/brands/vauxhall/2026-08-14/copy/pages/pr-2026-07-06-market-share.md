Note the leaderboard grammar. Three of six bullets are a **superlative claim narrowed by a
qualifier**: `UK's best-selling supermini year-to-date`, `UK's best-selling B-segment brand
year-to-date`, `UK's best-selling compact electric family SUV`. The qualifier does the legal
work in place of an asterisk. `year-to-date` is spelled out and hyphenated, never `YTD`.

## Body structure (FETCHER PARAPHRASE, structural only)

Eight paragraphs excluding bullets and boilerplate: (1) half-year share, registrations, volume
and retail growth; (2) ranking movement sixth to fourth, framed as progress toward a top-three
position; (3) industry-body data on B-segment leadership plus Frontera registrations and
segment placing; (4) electric retail performance driven by Frontera Electric; (5) overall
electric car growth in units and percentage points; (6) Corsa as volume leader with share and
unit totals; (7) light commercial vehicle growth in volume, units and share gain; (8) the
managing director's statement, across two paragraphs.

Read structurally: the release front-loads seven paragraphs of third-person verified numbers
and puts the only interpretive, emotional and forward-looking language in a quote at the end.
That is the house structure for corporate news.
