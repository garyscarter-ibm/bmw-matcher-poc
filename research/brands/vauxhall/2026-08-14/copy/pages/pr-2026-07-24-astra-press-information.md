## Headline (EXACT)

## Heading casing — mixed, and the mix is systematic

In document order, as written:

1. `Key Facts` — Title Case
2. `Over 45 years of heritage` — Sentence case
3. `Seven generations of Astra` — Sentence case (the body text describes eight generations, so
   the heading and the copy disagree inside one document)
4. `Design`
5. `Interior`
6. `Comfort and Practicality` — Title Case
7. `PureConnect: Advanced convenience` — brand prefix, colon, Sentence case
8. `PureSense: Intelligent safety`
9. `Front and rear parking distance sensors`
10. `Adaptive cruise control`
11. `Intelli-Drive 1.0`
12. `Advanced lighting: Intelli-Lux HD`
13. `PureEnergy: Exciting and efficient powertrains`
14. `Astra Electric`
15. `Astra Plug-in Hybrid`
16. `Astra Hybrid`
17. `Petrol`
18. `Charging`
19. `Astra Electric charging times`
20. `Astra Plug-in Hybrid charging times`
21. `On-the-road` — hyphenated
22. `Specification and pricing`
23. `GRIFFIN` / `GS (in addition to Griffin)` / `ULTIMATE (in addition to GS)` — ALL CAPS trim
    blocks, with colon-terminated sub-heads `Exterior styling:`, `PureSense:`,
    `PureConnect:`, `Comfort & convenience:`
24. `Technical specification` / `Power, performance and efficiency`
25. `Weights`, `Exterior dimensions`, `Luggage capacity (litres)`

27. `Electric Streets` — Title Case
28. `Electrification`
29. `Ownership`
30. `ENDS`
31. `ABOUT VAUXHALL MOTORS`

The pattern: **sentence case is the default for editorial headings; ALL CAPS is reserved for
trim-level names, `ENDS` and the boilerplate heading; the `Pure*` technology families take a
colon and then a sentence-case benefit phrase.** Bullets inside trim blocks are sentence case
with no terminal punctuation.

Note the last three labels: `Attainable:`, `ECG:`, `Accessible electric:`. Affordability is
given the same structural weight as power and range, and the label words are the brand's own
positioning vocabulary rather than neutral spec categories. `Sustainable:` and `Heritage:`
work the same way.

Also note `Curly apostrophes and single quotation marks` throughout (`driver’s`,
`‘Carbon Black’`), the ampersand inside bullets (`Heated front seats & steering wheel`,
`front & rear parking sensors`), `+` used as "and" (`electric + massage seats`), `=` used in
the charging bullet, and an Oxford comma in `Griffin, GS, and top-of-the-range Ultimate` and
`fully electric, plug-in hybrid, 48V hybrid, and 1.2-litre petrol options`.

## Technical figure conventions (EXACT examples)

Units close up to the numeral with no space. Thousands take commas. Ranges take hyphens.

1. `156hp electric motor` in prose, `Electric 156PS` in the spec table. **Both hp and PS are
   in use, prose versus table.**
2. `produce 270Nm of torque`
3. `a total output of 195hp and 360Nm of torque`
4. `enabling 0-62mph in 7.6 seconds (7.7 seconds for Sports Tourer)`
5. `both models have a top speed of 105mph`
6. `up to 281 miles of range for the hatchback, or 275 miles for the Sports Tourer (WLTP)`
7. `new 58kWh battery (4kWh larger than before)`
8. `100kW DC rapid charging, enabling a 20-80% recharge in just 32 minutes`
9. `in 3h 20min, or 8 hours via a normal 7kW home charger`
10. `achieving fuel economy of up to 48.7mpg (WLTP Combined High)`
11. CO2 column headed `CO2 (g/km)` with entries `108-112 (hatch);` and `49-52`
12. `up to 1,339 litres of luggage` / `load volume of up to 1,634 litres`
13. Dimensions in bare millimetres: `4,374mm`, `1,859mm`, `1,441mm`, `2,675mm`
14. Efficiency `4.1-4.2 miles/kWh`; weight column headed
    `Kerb weight with 75kg driver (kg)` with entry `1,733`
15. Aerodynamics `0.269 (cd)`; optional charger priced inline as `(£500)`

**Miles, mph and mpg throughout. No kilometres, no litres-per-100km.** The only metric units
are the engineering ones the industry uses natively: mm, kg, Nm, kWh, kW, g/km, litres of
boot space.

**Naming architecture, stated plainly**: model name plus powertrain word as a proper noun
(`Astra Electric`, `Combo Electric`, `Vivaro Electric`, `Astra Plug-in Hybrid`). There is no
`e-Corsa` or `e-Astra` form anywhere in the sample. Performance sub-brand is `GSE`, always
uppercase, always postfixed (`Corsa GSE`, `Mokka GSE`, `Mokka GSE Rally`). Technology is
`Pure` plus a capitalised noun, mostly closed up. Driver-assistance is `Intelli-` plus a
hyphen.

## Punctuation notes recorded by the fetcher

En dash `–` for parenthetical asides and ranges in prose. Curly apostrophes and single
quotes. Ratios as `40:20:40` and `60/40`. UK spellings (`colours`, `centre`, `kerb`).
`0-62mph` always hyphenated with no spaces.
