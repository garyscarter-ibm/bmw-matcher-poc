## What this tells the voice read

The parent's abstraction (`mobility provider`, `electric equality for all`) does not appear in
Vauxhall's own press releases at all. Vauxhall's own boilerplate says the same thing in concrete
terms instead: `attainable`, `every model in its car and van line-up`, `on-street electric
vehicle charging`. That gap between parent abstraction and brand concreteness is the single
clearest channel shift in the evidence set.

`Energising a Better Britain` likewise appears **nowhere** in the ten Vauxhall press artefacts
sampled. It is a Stellantis-level line for Vauxhall, not an observed Vauxhall sign-off. Flag it
as such: it is A2 first-party from the parent, and it is unverified as a line Vauxhall itself
uses in market.
