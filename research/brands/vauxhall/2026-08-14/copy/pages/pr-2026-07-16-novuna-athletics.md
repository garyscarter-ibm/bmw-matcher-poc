## Date and headline (EXACT)

> 16 Jul 2026

> VAUXHALL ANNOUNCED AS OFFICIAL AUTOMOBILE PARTNER OF THE NOVUNA LONDON ATHLETICS MEET

Note `AUTOMOBILE PARTNER`, not "automotive partner", for this deal, while the Team GB deal is
consistently `official automotive partner of Team GB` in the boilerplate. Two different
partner-title formulas, presumably contractual.

## Summary bullets (EXACT)

> - Vauxhall becomes Official Automobile Partner of the Novuna London Athletics Meet
> - Vauxhall becomes the title sponsor of the iconic Emsley Carr Mile
> - Partnership builds on Vauxhall's ongoing support of British sport through Team GB
> - Novuna London Athletics Meet takes place at the London Stadium on Saturday 18 July as part of the Wanda Diamond League

Date format inside a bullet: `Saturday 18 July`, day name then bare numeral, no ordinal suffix,
no comma. That differs from the small-print convention (`8th to 31st May`) and from the index
date stamp (`16 Jul 2026`). **Three date formats coexist across registers.**

Partner titles take Title Case as proper nouns (`Official Automobile Partner`,
`title sponsor` lower case). `iconic` used as an adjective for a heritage race, matching
`our iconic hot hatches` in the Corsa GSE quote: `iconic` is a favoured word.

conventional UK corporate warmth rather than distinctive voice.

## Body coverage (FETCHER PARAPHRASE, structural only)

Confirms the partnership and the naming rights to the invitational mile. Notes the meeting's
place in the Diamond League calendar, Vauxhall stadium branding and a trackside `Astra`
display, then previews the field: `Josh Kerr` chasing a sub-222-second world record, with
`Yared Nuguse`, `Nathan Green`, `Mohamed Abdilaahi`, `Robert Farken` and `Neil Gourley`.
Broadcast details on BBC One/iPlayer. Editor's notes cover the race's 70-plus-year history and
its namesake, a former newspaper editor. Then the standard boilerplate: 1903, Ellesmere Port
electric-only plant, charging campaign, Team GB ahead of Los Angeles 2028, Stellantis.

Note that the product placement in the copy is a car **on display at a sports event**, and the
only vehicle named is the mainstream `Astra`, not the performance `GSE`.
