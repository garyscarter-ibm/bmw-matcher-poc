(`Torsen®`, `Alcon®`); `from just £` as the price hedge.

## Footnotes and disclaimers (EXACT)

> ENDS

> *Pending government approval

That single asterisk is the entire caveat on a headline price claim: the £32,995 figure
depends on the government Electric Car Grant, and the release hangs the whole hedge on four
words. This is the cleanest example in the set of Vauxhall's asterisk convention.

## Body copy — figure and unit conventions (EXACT fragments)

- `281PS (207kW)` and `345Nm` — PS first, kW parenthetical, no space before unit.
- `0-62mph in 5.5 seconds` — hyphen, no spaces, mph closed up.
- `181hp/tonne`; `231PS/170kW`; top speed `112mph` reduced to `93mph` in Eco.
- `54kWh` gross, `51kWh` usable; `232 miles WLTP`; `11kW` on-board charger.
- `0-80% in 30 minutes` on a `100kW` DC charger; `0-100% in 5h45m` on 11kW AC; about
  `7h30m` on 7kW AC.
- Chassis deltas as absolute millimetres and percentages: ride height down `24mm` front /
  `11mm` rear; tracks up `54mm` / `20mm`; spring rates up `67%` front and `29%` rear;
  `30.8mm` anti-roll bar giving a `153%` rise in rear anti-roll stiffness; `14.5` rack ratio.
- `355mm front brake discs`; `18-inch` wheels (word "inch", not a prime symbol).
- Tyre range figures given per tyre: Michelin Pilot Sport 4S `220 miles`, Hankook Ventus S1
  evo3 `232 miles`.
- Paint upcharge as `£650`; pricing table: `VED £10 year one / £200 thereafter`,
  `BiK 4% (26/27)`, `P11D £34,430`, `OTR £34,495`, `£32,995` after ECG.
