# Range and model architecture, with exact orthography

Rung 2 (first-party dated press releases) for orthography; rung 5 (Wikipedia) for the
range table. Confidence: high for orthography, medium for range completeness.

## Adjudication rule applied

Per the brief, first-party dated usage wins for orthography. Wikipedia's "Current model
range" table (s43, article last edited 2025-10-27) contains **no electrified variant
naming at all**: the fetcher reported the only electric references in that section are
image filenames (a "Mokka-e" and a "Grandland GS EV" photo). First-party 2026 releases
do carry the electrified names. So Wikipedia is used for range membership only, and is
treated as **stale for orthography**.

**The convention is `<Model> Electric`, as a separate capitalised word.** No instance of
`Corsa-e`, `Mokka-e`, `Vivaro-e` or `e-Corsa` appears in any 2026 body text collected.
The hyphenated `-e` form survives only in a Wikipedia image filename (s43) and in a
third-party dealer's image path (s57), i.e. it is **legacy**, not current. Note the
limit of this evidence: no 2026 release collected here happens to name the electric
Corsa or electric Frontera in body text, so `Corsa Electric` and `Frontera Electric`
are **inferred from the pattern, not observed**. Flagged as a gap.

## Range membership (s43, Wikipedia, dated 2025-10-27)

Passenger cars, each recorded as a rebadge of a sibling: Astra (Opel Astra; earlier an
Opel Kadett rebadge, 1980 to present), Corsa (1993 to present), Mokka (2012 to present),
Grandland (2017 to present), Combo Life (Peugeot Rifter / Citroen Berlingo, 2018 to
present), Vivaro Life (Peugeot Traveller / Citroen SpaceTourer, 2019 to present),
Frontera (2024 to present).

Commercial vehicles: Combo (Peugeot Partner / Citroen Berlingo rebadge, previously Opel
Combo then Fiat Doblo, 1993 to present), Movano (Fiat Ducato based, previously Renault
Master, 1998 to present), Vivaro (Peugeot Expert / Citroen Jumpy, previously Renault
Trafic, 2001 to present). Body types given as van, chassis cab, crew cab.

Additions evidenced first-party but absent from that table: a new C-segment SUV for
Europe, sales expected from 2028, built on Leapmotor architecture, "under evaluation"
for the Corsa plant in Zaragoza, Spain (s54, 2026-05-08).

## Trim and badge conventions

Observed 2026, first-party:

- **Astra ladder: `Griffin`, `GS`, `Ultimate`** (s44, 2026-07-24). Also `Grandland
  Griffin` (press index, 2026-04-29) and `New Mokka Yes` (press index, 2026-07-28),
  so `Griffin` is an entry-level trim name reusing the marque's emblem name, and `Yes`
  is a special-edition trim name.
- **Van ladder: `Pro`, `Prime`, `Prime-Plus`** (s46, 2026-05-14), e.g. "Combo Electric
  Panel Van M 136hp Pro", "Vivaro Electric Panel Van M 136hp Prime", "Combo Electric
  Panel Van M 136hp Prime-Plus". Van size letters appear as `M` and `XL`.
- **`Design` was NOT observed** in any 2026 release collected, despite being named in
  this run's brief. Recorded as not found in current first-party usage, not as absent.
- Powertrain designations are written into the variant string: "Electric 156PS",
  "Plug-in Hybrid 1.6 195PS e-DCT7", "Hybrid 1.2 145PS e-DCT6", "1.2 130PS Turbo Auto"
  (s44). Gearbox codes are lower-case `e` plus caps: `e-DCT7`, `e-DCT6`.
- Feature-package names: `Vauxhall PureSense`, `Vauxhall PureConnect` (s53),
  `Intelli-Lux HD` (s44), `FlexCargo` bulkhead (s46), `Vauxhall Connect` and
  `MyVauxhall` app (s57).

## Segment vocabulary, first-party

`C-segment` ("the important and highly competitive C-segment"), `B-segment` ("the Corsa
is Vauxhall's B-segment icon"), `C-SUV` (s54, 2026-05-08). Vauxhall uses European
letter-segment vocabulary in its own copy. Contrast with SMMT, which does not publish a
body-style segment table at all (see `smmt-registrations-and-segments.md`).

## Gaps

- No observed body-text instance of the electric Corsa or electric Frontera name.
- No first-party naming document. Orthography here is induced from usage across five
  dated releases, not read off a rule.
- Full current price list and full trim matrix per model sit on vauxhall.co.uk, walled.
